How protection works

  • The file is encrypted with AES-256, which works in all current PDF readers.
  • There’s no way to recover a forgotten password, so keep it somewhere safe. PDFLab never sees or stores it.
  • Printing, copying and editing restrictions are respected by most readers (like Acrobat), but some apps ignore them. Only the open password truly keeps people out.
