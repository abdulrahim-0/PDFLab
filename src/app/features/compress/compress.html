What compression can do here

  • Photos inside the PDF are re-compressed, and scaled down at stronger levels.
  • Text, fonts and drawings are kept exactly as they are, so they stay sharp.
  • Scanned and photo-heavy PDFs shrink the most. Text-only PDFs are usually already small and may not change.
  • If the result isn’t smaller, you get your original file back.
